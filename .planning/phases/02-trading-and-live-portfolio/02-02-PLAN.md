<objective>
Make the portfolio visible and live. The header shows Total, Cash and P&L since start; a positions table and a heatmap fill the two Phase 2 panels; all three recompute from the stored positions times the SSE prices on every frame, without polling. Clicking a position row or tile charts that ticker, and that selection survives watchlist reloads even when the ticker is held but not watched.

Purpose: success criterion 3 (live header, positions, heatmap, empty heatmap text, click to chart) and the client half of criterion 2 (a sold-out position vanishes at once, because the store applies the trade response).

Output: the pure live valuation and treemap modules, `PositionsTable`, `Heatmap`, the extended `Header`, and held-aware chart selection.

User story note (MVP mode): the ROADMAP Goal line is not in user-story format; no story is invented here.
</objective>
