<objective>
Close the two gaps in 02-VERIFICATION.md so that every invalid order returns a 400 with the server's message, and a malformed body returns a 422, instead of a 500 or an unintended fill.

1. **CR-01 (blocker).** A huge finite quantity (about 1e24 or more) escapes `TradeService` as an unhandled `decimal.InvalidOperation` and returns 500. Quantities from about 1e23 up to 1e24 can also overflow `notional` inside the trade transaction. The fix adds a `MAX_QUANTITY` cap that runs before any Decimal work, plus backstops that turn a decimal error or an int-to-float overflow into a `DomainError`. That restores the "rule failures raise DomainError" contract the Phase 3 AI executor depends on.
2. **WR-03.** `TradeRequest.quantity` is a lax float, so JSON `true` buys 1 share and `"10"` buys 10. The fix makes the field `StrictFloat`, which returns 422 for those bodies. It also adds a matching type gate in the service for callers that bypass the request model.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 2 success criterion 2, TRD-01 and TRD-06 become fully true. The verifier marked them FAILED (edge) and PARTIAL.
Output: the cap and backstops in `trading.py`, `StrictFloat` in `api/portfolio.py`, a precision-range note in `money.py`, and tests at the service, route and trade-bar levels.
