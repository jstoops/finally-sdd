<objective>
Bring the portfolio into the terminal and make the header live. The terminal loads the portfolio on mount and after a stream reconnect; the header shows Total, Cash and P&L since start, recomputed on every SSE frame from the stored positions times the streamed prices, without polling, and matching the server to the cent. Chart selection learns about held tickers, so a held but unwatched ticker can stay charted.

Purpose: success criterion 1's "cash moves by exactly the trade cost" on screen, success criterion 3's live header, and the selection groundwork for criterion 3's click-to-chart (the rows and tiles that click arrive in 02-04).

Output: `loadPortfolio`, `refresh` and the sequence guard in `usePortfolio`, the shared `stubApi` test helper, `livePortfolio` and the cent and sign helpers, the live `Header`, and held-aware `nextSelection` with `useWatchlist.setHeld`.
