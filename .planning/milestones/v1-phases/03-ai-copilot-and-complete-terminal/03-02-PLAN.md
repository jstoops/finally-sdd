<objective>
Make every chat reply safe and honest. The parser accepts what the model really sends (fences, missing arrays, extra keys) and fails cleanly on garbage; the executor validates each item on its own, caps each list at 10, keeps running after a failure and writes a templated "Couldn't ..." note for each one; the service rejects empty or over-long messages before storing anything, builds the real FinAlly prompt from the live portfolio and the last 20 messages, serializes turns with a chat-only lock, and serves the last 50 messages at `GET /api/chat/history`.

Purpose: success criterion 2 (failed actions carry a warning note while the rest still execute), criterion 3's 400/502 and mock rules, criterion 4's history, and the backend half of criterion 1 ("Analyze my portfolio" has the real numbers in its prompt). The tracer (03-01) proved the path; this plan fills in its rules without changing any interface the frontend or the LLM client depend on.

Output: final `parsing.py`, `actions.py` and `service.py`, the new `prompt.py`, the history route, and the backend chat test suite.
